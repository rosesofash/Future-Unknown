# Sound effects

- `soundscape.mp3`: quiet cinematic soundscape, loops in the background from the start, and again after the rescue.
- `waves.mp3`: crashing waves, loops at the lifeboats, until the line starts to fade.
- `sonar.mp3`: the ping during the fading line and the yellow beacon.

Volumes are at the top of `js/story.js`. Cite every effect you use in the project's sources (your PDF).
