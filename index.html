Future: Unknown

A documentary by Anjena Anandakumar

My father left Sri Lanka because of the war. In 1986, he was one of 155 Tamil refugees who set out from West Germany on a cargo ship, not knowing where they would end up.

This is his journey, told in his own voice and traced as a single dotted line. At each stop you'll hear him remember it, and news reports and records from the time fill in the rest.

  • Click each stop to hear him
  • Sound on. Headphones help
  • About six minutes, or use Skip to end (top right) to explore at your own pace
